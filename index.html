In Spotify, tap Share → Copy Link, then paste it here. Pause freezes the countdown and lets you resume from the same position; Stop ends the cue and resets the next Play to its saved starting point. Fade lowers volume and stops the cue at the end. A red Played check appears when the playback request succeeds; Reset Played clears them all. Optionally enter an automatic fade start time (e.g. 2:30 means at 2:30 into that song). Fade timing depends on Spotify and the selected device. Cue points are saved on this device. Local audio files remain in the browser's IndexedDB and are not included in JSON backups. Example: 1:15 starts 75 seconds into the track.

Important limitations

This is a personal-use prototype. Spotify device support varies: some devices cannot fade remotely and will pause instead. It does not bypass Spotify Premium, download songs, or provide sample-accurate instant audio. Fade out uses stepped Spotify Connect volume changes, not a locally rendered audio fade. It may be uneven or unavailable on some devices. If volume changes fail, the app still attempts to pause the song. Automatic fade timing requires this page to remain open and active; background iPad tabs can delay it. Spotify may reject playback controls on some devices. iPad volume cannot be controlled directly by browser JavaScript; if Spotify reports supports_volume=false, Fade uses Pause instead. Playback device IDs can expire and must be refreshed. Spotify API quotas and platform policies apply. Low-API Event Mode avoids regular Spotify countdown polling. The locally estimated timer can drift if tracks are paused or changed outside the Cue Board; use Check player once to resynchronise. Spotify-based fades still require multiple remote volume commands. When Spotify reports 429, all Spotify API requests pause for the required cooldown. Do not rely on it for a live show until tested with your specific iPad and output device.
